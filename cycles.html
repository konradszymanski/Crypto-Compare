How this comparison works

Cycles begin on Bitcoin halving dates: Nov 28, 2012; Jul 9, 2016; May 11, 2020; and Apr 20, 2024. The selected date determines the elapsed days in the current cycle; earlier cycles are compared at that same day, capped at their next halving. A cycle is included for an asset only when daily prices exist within seven days after its halving. CoinGecko USD history is tried first. If it is unavailable or rate limited, the chart falls back to Binance BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT daily spot candles; that fallback covers the 2020 and 2024 cycles, not the older halvings. The latest in-progress UTC daily candle is excluded.

Historical data: CoinGecko or Binance Spot. Binance USDT prices are a stablecoin proxy for USD. For research and comparison only.
