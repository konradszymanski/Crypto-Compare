How to read this

Market context, not a signal

Global market figures and the top 100 by market capitalization describe only the assets CoinGecko tracks and the periods shown. They do not predict what happens next. Trending search and category endpoints require an authenticated CoinGecko Demo key according to its current documentation; this static site will not expose a key in browser code. Compare this snapshot with historical halving cycles and the event timeline to inspect context from multiple angles.

Market data: CoinGecko. USD values are snapshots and may be delayed. Research and comparison only.
